Privacy Policy for Tab Audio Recorder

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Tab Audio Recorder stores your selected interface language and audio format on your device. These settings are not uploaded.

When you start a recording, the extension captures the audio playing in the current Chrome tab and reads the tab title to label the recording and suggest a filename. It captures all audio playing in that tab. The extension does not access your microphone or read your browsing history list.

The extension records audio, holds it temporarily in memory, and converts it to your selected MP3 or WAV format on your device. If conversion fails, the extension can try to save the original WebM recording. Chrome's download feature saves the file on your device when you choose to save it. The extension does not upload, sell, or share your recordings or tab titles with the developer or third parties. It does not use analytics or advertising services.

Unsaved recordings are retained temporarily during the browser session and may be lost when Chrome closes or the extension is removed. You can manage and delete downloaded recordings on your device. Chrome manages its own download history.

The extension's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

网页音频提取器隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月11日

网页音频提取器由发布者运营。本政策适用于该 Chrome 扩展。

处理的数据

扩展会在本机保存您选择的界面语言和音频格式。这些设置不会上传。

当您主动开始录制时，扩展读取当前浏览器标签页的音频，并读取该标签页标题，用于在界面显示和生成下载文件名。录制的是标签页的全部音频，可能包括除目标视频之外的声音。扩展不会访问您的麦克风。

音频在您的设备上录制、暂存在内存，并转换为您选择的 MP3 或 WAV。转换失败时，扩展会尝试保存原始 WebM。扩展通过 Chrome 下载功能将您选择保存的文件写入您的设备。扩展不读取您的浏览历史列表，也不向开发者或第三方服务器上传、出售或共享音频或标签页标题。扩展没有分析或广告服务。

未保存的录音仅在浏览器会话中暂存；开始新的录音前可保存或放弃。关闭浏览器、扩展被卸载或浏览器意外退出可能丢失暂存内容。已下载的文件由您在设备上管理和删除。Chrome 自身的下载记录由浏览器管理。

扩展对从 Google API 接收的信息的使用遵守 Chrome 网上应用店用户数据政策，包括有限使用要求。
